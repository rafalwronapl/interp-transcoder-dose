The earlier exposure and morphology findings have been addressed. The expanded manifest contains 6,692 source files. Their bytes matched the declared hashes during review. Every historical Python source plus the additional 227 labelled text/data sources supplements the original inventory. The production qualification handles known irregular forms, lowercases aliases before screening, excludes historical aliases and their regular plurals, and selects one representative from connected shared-noun-synset components. Reproduction agrees with the actual pool. No retained noun synsets overlap between selected representatives; no screened literal/regular-plural aliases occur in the historical observed-word set. Names are exclusive to the NLTK female/male categories and removed when they duplicate selected noun words. These labels are corpus categories, not guarantees about people's gender or a population study of name effects.

A further countability audit found genuine shortcomings in WordNet's article-based glosses. Merriam-Webster gives [vermin an invariant plural](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/vermin) and identifies [talaria as a plural noun](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/talaria). Cambridge marks [cellophane](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/cellophane) and [cookware](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/cookware?topic=kitchen-utensils) uncountable; Oxford similarly classifies [cling film](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/cling-film). The premeasurement denylist now excludes these and eight additional doubtful mass, collective or invariant forms, including their connected candidate components. These extra exclusions are conservative eligibility choices; they do not assert that all such words lack any count sense. Exact reasons and source links are preserved in `countability_exclusions_v2.json`.

The final qualified pool has 2,225 nouns, 3,646 female-category names and 1,225 male-category names. The draft uses 2,752 distinct casefolded lexical forms across 1,376 blocks and 14,848 documents. The A allocation has sixteen independent FIT cohorts, each with sixteen FIT_MAP, sixteen FIT_U and sixteen FIT_EXT blocks; the designated primary realization is r0. Shared A PILOT and maximum TEST have 32 and 256 blocks. B has 32 FIT, 32 PILOT and 256 maximum TEST pairs. No lexical forms repeat across these blocks. This review checks allocation integrity, not the scientific sufficiency of sixteen cohorts or the choice of N.

All donor checks passed: donors remain in the same block, frame and distractor condition and reverse the target side; the pairing is involutive. Tokenizer replay checked every one of the 14,848 literal prompts and both answer-token pairs, with no differences. Donor sequence lengths may differ: interventions use the final prompt position in each donor and recipient independently, as declared. A positionwise MLP edit does not require equal sequence length. That observation does not replace model-stage hook and suffix-parity controls.
