# B PILOT acquisition-only amendment: narrow design judgment

2026-11-01. Independent reviewer: /root/astra_goals_and_concurrency_review.

Scientifically acceptable in principle: a prospectively frozen32-block PILOT can measure nuisance variation and technical/descriptive behavior before primary-family calibration qualifies. Collecting measurements does not require that the planned inferential procedure already have demonstrated its validity. The acquisition must remain useful as a retained descriptive/nuisance dataset even if the family gate ultimately prevents confirmatory continuation. This is not authorization to launch or a finding that B1–B3 are supported.

The existing family failure remains a failure. Passing the new RMS precision calibration does not qualify the distinct primary hypothesis family. Do not replace the family eligibility flag, recast a failed case as optional, or claim that acquisition implies inferential eligibility.

Required scope of any new literal packet:

- PILOT only, exact original32-block corpus prefix, unchanged frozen FIT/TC, directions, operators, doses, baselines and readouts. No refitting, replacement blocks, new outcome-driven selection or TEST acquisition.
- Actual accepted technical FIT and tail fidelity evidence; actual accepted new precision qualification if this is the stated prerequisite. Reuse accepted evidence, without another simulation or numerical-method review.
- Explicit objective: nuisance/descriptive acquisition without primary-support decisions. Publish or retain the data and negative/undefined technical findings even if the family remains ineligible. No inference that a nonsignificant descriptive contrast demonstrates no effect or equivalence.
- Preserve existing family-failure provenance. No selection of an easier scientific hypothesis, frame, method or threshold using observed PILOT signs. Any later scientific/inferential amendment is separate and prospective; this packet promises none.
- A distinct pilot-only semantic admission, fixed schedule and resource budget, independent pre-run acceptance, unchanged cache/operation accounting. No generic authorization bypass, no TEST-capable override and no inference-enabled downstream authorization.
- Keep current A measurements and caches unchanged. Concurrent model capacity, available memory and locks require operational handling separately; this design judgment does not approve a changed dtype/model or risk an unreviewed concurrent launch.

The benefit is earlier acquisition of already prescribed nuisance/technical evidence. The limitation is material: it may never lead to confirmatory B TEST under the current family method. If the dataset has no intended descriptive use and continuation is impossible under the retained protocol, acquisition would only spend resources; concurrency alone is not a scientific objective.

No tests, simulations or model calls performed for this judgment. Existing frozen scientific definitions and prior technical/design reviews are reused. This is conditional design advice only; no additional maintenance stage is requested.
