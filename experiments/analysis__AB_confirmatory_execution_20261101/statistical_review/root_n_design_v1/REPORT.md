## Accepted before-freeze gates

**NS1 medium — synchronize the executable specification.** Current sample_size_spec.json still says covariance/dispersion/cost only and retains an L justification linking squared0.20logit to a roughly5percentage-point binary change. That statement is not a valid interpretation of a general0.04MSE difference. The reviewed root draft explicitly corrects this. Accepted for a direction-only review because the root draft openly establishes this gate and does not claim current executable readiness.

**NS2 medium — coherent B power generator remains unresolved.** Current validate_family_v2 generates the B2 energy tuple separately from the sampled B1/B3 mean-contrast noise. It can assess its stated synthetic null configurations but cannot establish readiness of a joint physical-unit B power generator preserving shared native/TC energies. The root draft explicitly requires a positive-energy model or estimator-level construction that imposes the fixed targets and preserves those dependencies; its formula and target agreement must be verified before PILOT. Do not center a scalar log-ratio and call it the production ratio test. Accepted as an explicit unresolved gate, not as an implemented solution.

**NS3 medium — approximation/production bridge remains unresolved.** precision.py computes the correct pooled RMS estimands and12 Bonferroni percentile intervals, but analytic_halfwidth is only a first-order design approximation. It has not been independently shown to conservatively approximate the actual joint production precision event. At20000bootstrap draws, each tail of the99%Bonferroni12 construction contains only about8.33draws on average (.01/24×20000). The bridge must assess joint widths, coverage and Monte Carlo instability in these sparse tails, not just scalar p-value agreement. The root draft correctly makes validation mandatory and allows direct production-method simulation only subject to a cost/feasibility review.

**NS4 medium — stability precision remains unresolved.** Sixteen independent FIT cohorts is a prospective candidate supported by the earlier stress screen, not a precision-certified choice. The root draft correctly requires a numerical between-FIT precision goal, crossed FIT×TEST calibration and cost assessment before the additional15cohorts. Their mean cannot replace primaryr0. ## Monte Carlo selection budget

There are at most6 acceptance probabilities: A joint power, B joint power and B joint precision assurance, each at2candidateN. Six **one-sided** Clopper–Pearson lower bounds at alpha=.05/6 provide simultaneous coverage at least.95 by the union bound, including selection of the smallest passing candidate. For k successes among R fixed replications, the lower bound is0 if k=0, otherwise beta_quantile(.05/6;k,R−k+1). The existing validation.binomial_bounds helper is two-sided; calling it with alpha=.05/6 would use .05/12 in the lower tail and therefore be more conservative. Do not describe that as the exact one-sided formula.

Failed/undefined simulation replicates must remain in the denominator and count as failures, as the root design specifies. Extra success probabilities used as acceptance gates need a new multiplicity allocation. Marginal powers may be descriptive reports without becoming new selection gates. A95%Monte Carlo confidence statement for these6selection probabilities is not automatically a95%statement for every calibration, bridge and stability gate together; separate confidence budgets must be described by scope. The design correctly does not reuse its6-test claim to certify unresolved extra gates.

Fixed10000replications is a proposal to freeze and cost before PILOT, not permission to adapt repetition counts to a favorable bound. The selectedN receipt should bind PILOT inputs, fixed code, seed map, eligibility receipts and generator formula, and reproduce without accessing TEST. These outputs and their independent review remain required.
